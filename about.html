ABOUT LAYERAI

Creative workflows, made easier.

LayerAI is an independent project focused on helpful image-editing tools, practical learning resources, and clearer explanations of AI-assisted creative work.

Our purpose

Creative software can feel complicated, especially when you are learning a new workflow. LayerAI aims to make the process easier to understand through approachable guides, experiments, and tools designed around real creative tasks.

What you can expect

  • Practical explanations of image editing and layered files.
  • Learning resources about AI-assisted design workflows.
  • Clear descriptions of what a tool can and cannot do.
Project status: Some tools and previews shown on this website are still in development. They should not be considered active services until clearly announced.
